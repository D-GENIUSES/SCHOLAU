BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY AND BUSINESS IDEAS 
Entrepreneurial opportunities are not equally obvious to everyone but are equally available to 
anyone with the experiences and knowledge to discover them. Opportunities are themselves 
unstructured where advantages and disadvantages of opportunities are largely dependent on 
individual differences in terms of personal experience, exposure, and training. 
Business Opportunity: A business opportunity may be described as an attractive proposition 
that provides the possibility of a return for the investor or the person taking the risk. 
Business Ideas: This is a matter of conception  which is a perceived or conscious thought of 
activity that may yield a return if carried out. It represents an investment opportunity that could 
lead to the creation of venture (s) 
Characteristics of a Good Business Opportunity 
To be a good business idea to be considered as a business opportunity, it must fulfil or be 
capable of fulfilling the following criteria; 
a. Real demand 
b. Return on Investment 
c. Competitiveness 
d. Veritable objectives 
e. Availability of resources and skills 
Generating Business Ideas 
Sequel to the above, a good business idea could be generated by sincerely and objectively 
answering these questions 
a. What product or service do you think as a business will sell? 
b. Who are the potential customers? 
c. Where is the intended location of your business? 
d. Which need do you want to fulfil? 
e. When do you intend to launch the product or service? 
f. How do you want to market the product or service? 
It is a general belief that these questions will enable you to sift different ideas and get a viable 
business idea worthy of investing upon.

FINANCIAL ASPECT OF ENTREPRENEURSHIP 
Finance is the management of money resource for a specific purpose by individuals, corporate 
bodies, and government. It is often a critical component to the business's success. 
Classification of Sources of Entrepreneurial Financing 
The sources of entrepreneurial financing can be classified into three ma in groups i.e., short -
term, medium-term, and long-term financing 
Short-term is usually between 0 – 12 months which can be under any of these friends and 
relatives, cooperatives, trade credits, overdrafts, factoring, acceptance credits etc., 
Medium-term is usually between 1 – 2 years and can be one of these commercial banks, hire 
purchase, leasing etc., 
Long-term is usually 3 years and above and can be one of these debentures, preference shares, 
ordinary shares, retained earnings etc.,

E-COMMERCE 
E-commerce is the abbreviation of electronic commerce. It is the buying and selling of products 
and services online.  It can involve transactions between individuals, businesses, households, 
or private organizations.  E-commerce can include  ordering goods and services, buying 
financial investments, confirming travel and accommodation reservations, paying for internet 
information services, and buying through online auctions. 
The Internet service is the foundation and critical to e-commerce activities.  The Internet allows 
individuals and businesses to buy and sell an increasing amount of physical goods, digital 
goods, and services electronically. 
How does ecommerce work? 
Ecommerce works by connecting sellers with customers and allowing exchanges to take place 
online. It can work in many different ways and take many forms. Here’s a general overview of 
how the process can look: 
1. The seller chooses online selling channels, like a website or social media, and 
promotes products or services for sale. 
2. Customers find the products or services and place orders. 
3. A payment processor enables the exchange of the goods or services electronically 
via payment options like credit cards or digital currencies. 
4. The customer receives a confirmation email or SMS along with a printable receipt. 
5. If the transaction is for goods, the seller ships the products and sends the customer a 
tracking number via email or SMS. If the transaction is for a service, the service 
provider can reach out to schedule and complete the service. 
Types of E-Commerce 
E-commerce takes as many different forms as there are various ways to interact with online 
channels. For example, sellers and buyers exchange goods and services through m-commerce, 
enterprise commerce, and social selling destinations 
A few common business models are: 
• B2C: Businesses sell to individual consumers, sometimes called the “end customer.” 
• B2B: Businesses sell to other businesses . Often the buyer  resells products  to the 
consumer. 
• C2B: Consumers sell to businesses. C2B businesses allow customers to sell to other 
companies. 
• C2C: Consumers sell to other consumers. Businesses create online shopping 
destinations to connect customers. 
• B2G: Businesses sell to governments or government agencies.

Steps in E-Commerce Business 
The steps to starting an ecommerce business can vary depending on factors like what you want 
to sell. For example, if you're selling services, then there’s no need to manage inventory or 
fulfillment. However, if you want to sell products online, then inventory and fulfillment will 
likely play a critical part in your operations. 
Here are some steps you can follow to get started: 
1. Research business ideas. 
2. Make sure there’s demand for the products you want to sell. 
3. Determine how you'll sell and ship products to customers. 
4. Find suppliers and manufacturers. 
5. Choose which online channels you'll sell through. 
6. Create a website or online storefront and list products. 
7. Create a plan for your fulfillment strategy. 
8. Begin attracting customers with promotions 
Benefits of e-commerce 
Below are some of the benefits of e-commerce 
1. Accessibility and convenience  
2. Direct access to customers 
3. Global marketing reach 
4. Relatively low operating costs 
5. Advances in technology 
Challenges of e-commerce 
Aside from the above benefits, here are some of the challenges of e-commerce 
1. High competition  
2. Limited interaction with customers and products 
3. Shipping and fulfillment at scale 
4. Reliance on technology 
5. Data security concerns

 ENTREPRENEURSHIP INNOVATION 
Entrepreneurship innovation is synonymous with Joseph Schumpeter through his work on 
destructive creati vity. Innovation in entrepreneurship means the practice of creating new 
business ideas and plans with the intention of generating profits, helping the community, and 
accompanying company goals i.e., the art of implementing new ideas, strategies, and thought 
processes with a vision to introduce or improve the present. 
In the long run, in order for a business to survive and grow, innovating a product or service 
becomes a crucial step, and as observed and experienced innovation in any business helps an 
entrepreneur to improve or restore a particular product, process, or service. 
Who is an Innovative Entrepreneur? 
An innovative entrepreneur is a person who has the potential of designing and bringing 
innovative products into the market. Innovative entrepreneur in the name itself reflects a 
responsibility over a person’s shoulder to generate and bring new ideas into business which 
can later be converted into life-changing products. 
Innovative entrepreneurs design business models to meet the corporate needs of businesses and 
improve their competitiveness in the market. Innovative entrepreneurs are individuals with 
innovative ideas to create long-lasting solutions for common problems shared by a large 
number of people or countries 
The Importance of Innovative Entrepreneurship 
It's important to have innovative entrepreneurship because  
1. It helps to identify new trends and market demand.  
2. It helps a company to produce new goods or services that appeal to its target audience. 
3. It helps to adapt to new trends in its industry.  
4. It helps to create innovative products, services or up to date brand development to stay 
relevant. 
Traits of Innovative Entrepreneurs  
Some successful innovative entrepreneurs have specific personality traits that contribute to 
their innovative ways of thinking. Personality traits for a successful innovative entrepreneur 
can include: 
1. They're patient 
2. They're proactive  
3. They're excellent communicators 
4. They're determined to find solutions to challenges 
5. They're okay with change
