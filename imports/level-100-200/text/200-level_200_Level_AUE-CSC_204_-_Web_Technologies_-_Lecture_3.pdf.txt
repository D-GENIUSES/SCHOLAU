What is "Semantics"?
AUE-CSC 204 – WEB TECHNOLOGIES – BALOGUN JEREMIAH ADEMOLA – DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE
•In linguistics, semantics is the study of meaning.
•In HTML, a Semantic Element is a tag that clearly describes its
content to both the browser and the developer.
•Non-Semantic Elements: <div> and <span>.
•They tell us nothing about what is inside them.
•They are just empty boxes.
•Semantic Elements: <header>, <article>, and <footer>.
•They tell the browser exactly what their role is.

•For the Computer Scientist (SEO): Search engines like Google use "web

•When you return to a project after 6 months, seeing <section id="results"> is
much clearer than seeing <div id="div12">

The Key HTML5 Structural Elements

Tag Purpose
<header> Introductory content, logos, and the main title.
<nav> A block of navigation links (menus).
<main> The unique, central content of the page (only one per page!).
<section> A thematic grouping of content, usually with its own heading.
<article> Independent, self-contained content (like a blog post or news story).
<aside> Tangentially related content (sidebars, ads, or "quick facts").
<footer> Copyright info, contact details, and site maps.

CSS Syntax: The "Command" Structure
AUE-CSC 204 – WEB TECHNOLOGIES – BALOGUN JEREMIAH ADEMOLA – DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE
•To style an element, you need three things: a
Selector (who), a Property (what), and a Value (how
much).
• Selector: Targets the HTML element (e.g., h1, .btn-
success, #main-header).
• Property: The feature you want to change (e.g., color,
font-size, margin).
• Value: The specific setting (e.g., blue, 24px, 10px).

The "Golden Rule": The CSS Box Model
AUE-CSC 204 – WEB TECHNOLOGIES – BALOGUN JEREMIAH ADEMOLA – DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE
• This is the most important concept in web layout.
• In CSS, everything is a box.
• Understanding how these boxes are sized is the difference between
a professional site and a broken one.
• Content: The actual text or image.
• Padding: The "inner space" between the content and the border. (Makes
buttons look "breathable").
• Border: The line surrounding the padding and content.
• Margin: The "outer space" between this box and its neighbors. (Prevents
elements from sticking together).
• The "Lagos Traffic" Analogy:
• Think of the Content as the people in a car, the Padding as the
interior space of the car, the Border as the car's body, and the
Margin as the "social distancing" space between your car and the
one in front of you in traffic!

--- page 16 ---
The Flex Concept: Parent and Child
AUE-CSC 204 – WEB TECHNOLOGIES – BALOGUN JEREMIAH ADEMOLA – DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE
•Flexbox works on a Parent-Child relationship.
• Flex Container: The parent element where you set
display: flex;.
• Flex Items: The immediate children of that container.
•Once you enable flex, you gain control over the Main
Axis (the direction items go) and the Cross Axis
(perpendicular to the main axis).

Property What it does Common Values

align-items Aligns items on the Cross Axis. flex-start, center, stretch (default)
flex-wrap Allows items to wrap to a new line. nowrap (default), wrap
